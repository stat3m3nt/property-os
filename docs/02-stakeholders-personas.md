# 1. Purpose

This document identifies the stakeholders and primary user personas for PropertyOS.

The purpose of this document is to define who interacts with the platform, their goals, responsibilities, pain points, and business needs.

Understanding these users ensures that future user stories, use cases, workflows, and interface designs are centered around real-world operational requirements.

---

# 2. Stakeholders

Stakeholders include individuals or organizations that influence, use, or benefit from PropertyOS.

---

# 2.1 Primary Stakeholders

## Property Management Companies

### Description

Organizations responsible for managing residential and commercial property portfolios on behalf of owners.

### Responsibilities

- Manage multiple properties
- Coordinate staff
- Monitor financial performance
- Handle maintenance operations

### Business Goals

- Improve operational efficiency
- Reduce administrative work
- Improve reporting
- Scale operations

---

## Independent Landlords

### Description

Individuals responsible for managing their own rental properties.

### Responsibilities

- Collect rent
- Manage leases
- Coordinate maintenance
- Communicate with tenants

### Business Goals

- Simplify property management
- Stay organized
- Reduce paperwork

---

## Property Managers

### Description

Employees responsible for daily property operations.

### Responsibilities

- Manage tenants
- Process maintenance requests
- Create leases
- Track occupancy

### Business Goals

- Save time
- Improve tenant satisfaction
- Complete daily work efficiently

---

# 2.2 Secondary Stakeholders

## Tenants

### Description

Individuals renting residential or commercial units.

### Responsibilities

- Pay rent
- Submit maintenance requests
- Communicate with management

### Goals

- Quick maintenance resolution
- Easy communication
- Access lease information

---

## Vendors / Contractors

### Description

External companies performing maintenance work.

### Responsibilities

- Complete work orders
- Update work status

### Goals

- Receive accurate job information
- Complete work efficiently

---

## Property Owners

### Description

Individuals or companies owning managed properties.

### Goals

- Maximize occupancy
- Increase profitability
- Receive accurate reports

---

## System Administrators

### Description

Personnel responsible for maintaining the platform.

### Goals

- Maintain security
- Manage users
- Ensure system reliability

---

The following personas represent typical users of PropertyOS.

# Persona 1 — Olivia Bennett

## Role

Property Manager

---

## Background

Olivia manages approximately 180 residential units across several apartment buildings.

She spends most of her day coordinating maintenance requests, communicating with tenants, creating leases, and monitoring occupancy.

---

## Goals

- Reduce repetitive administrative work
- Quickly locate tenant information
- Monitor maintenance requests
- Track vacant units
- Generate reports

---

## Pain Points

- Too many spreadsheets
- Information spread across multiple systems
- Time-consuming manual updates
- Poor visibility into maintenance progress

---

## Technology Experience

High

Uses property management software daily.

---

## Frequency of Use

Daily

Approximately 6–8 hours.

---

## Primary Features

- Dashboard
- Properties
- Units
- Maintenance
- Reports
- Tenants
- Leases
